Direction B

Field journal

Why

Warm paper, ink-black text, one forest green, a serif for headings and numbers — a naturalist's record book rather than a machine console. The photo dominates: filmstrip along the bottom, one measurement panel on the right. Easy on the eyes in a lit office, and it prints and screenshots into a report without looking alien.

Trade-off

A bottom filmstrip shows ~9 photos at a glance where a side list shows 12–14, and scanning a 400-photo folder means a lot of horizontal travel. Light on a bright monitor also flattens the dark parts of a night photo.
