Direction C

Data console

Why

Near-black with an electric lime accent, monospace throughout, an icon rail instead of tabs. The table is the main object: every photo is a row with its distance, interval, confidence and alignment, sortable and filterable, with the frame in a preview pane. Built for someone working through 400 photos, not 11.

Trade-off

The photo stops being the hero — the preview pane is small, so verifying a box means expanding, which is the opposite of what you asked for. Monospace everywhere is loud, and the lime is unforgiving over a bright frame.
