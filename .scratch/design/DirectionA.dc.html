Why

A three-pane workbench, like a measuring device rather than a web page: graphite chrome, one amber accent for anything you can act on, tabular numerals everywhere. List on the left, frame in the middle, numbers on the right — nothing scrolls but the list, and the photo is always the largest thing on screen.

Trade-off

Three panes means the frame is ~730 px wide on a 1440 window — plenty to judge a box, not enough to study fine texture. Dense and technical: it looks like a tool for a specialist, not something a visitor picks up.
